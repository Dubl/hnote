Song 2 · Tree

50 beats — the hierarchy is the rhythm

No grid. Each beat is a nested ratio tree: the 8s loop divides by funky shares (3-3-2 tresillo, 2-1-1, 3-1...), each segment divides again, 4-5 levels deep — and the voices speak the tree's levels: kick = level 1, snare = level 2 (preferring onsets off the kicks), ride = the deep level, ghost colors = the leaves. Everything locks because it's one tree; nothing squares because the tree isn't square. Swing is a lean applied at any node, at that node's scale. 69% of onsets are off any grid; 97% of snares are syncopated. 2 bars = 16.0s/unit. 13.3 min, drums only.
